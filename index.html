Skincare Routines
6-Week Treatment
Not started yet
☀️ Morning Now
  • Wash your face with a gentle cleanser.
  • Apply Dermaglós Emulsión Hidratante if your skin feels dry.
  • Apply Idraet Protector Solar FPS 50 con Color (tinted) generously — two-finger rule.
  • If outdoors for more than ~2 hours, reapply sunscreen. Reapplication is key with melasma treatment.
🚿 After Shower Now
  • Dry your face gently — don't rub.
  • If sunscreen washed off, apply Dermaglós Emulsión if needed.
  • Reapply Idraet Protector Solar FPS 50 con Color (tinted) generously — two-finger rule. Only if your shower is at noon or in the afternoon.
🌙 Night Now
  • Cleanse your face gently.
  • Wait 15–20 minutes so skin is completely dry. This reduces irritation from hydroquinone.
  • Apply Melasmax only on the brown patches — thin layer.
  • Wait about 10 minutes.
  • If surrounding skin feels dry, apply Dermaglós Emulsión around face, avoiding the Melasmax-treated areas.

Track how long you keep this going 💪
☀️ Morning Now
  • Wash your face with a gentle cleanser.
  • Apply Idraet Pro White Serum (Niacinamide + Chromabright®).
  • Allow it to absorb for 1–2 minutes.
  • Apply Idraet Protector Solar FPS 50 con Color (tinted) generously — two-finger rule.
  • If outdoors for more than 2 hours, reapply sunscreen.
🚿 After Shower Now
  • Pat your face dry — don't rub.
  • If skin feels dry, apply Dermaglós Emulsión Hidratante.
  • Apply Idraet Protector Solar FPS 50 con Color again before going outside. Only if your shower is at noon or in the afternoon.
🌙 Night Now
  • Wash your face with a gentle cleanser.
  • Apply Acm Azéane Crema Ácido Azelaico Al 15% Every other night for the first 2 weeks, then every night if well tolerated.
  • Wait 10–15 minutes (optional but recommended when layering actives).
  • Apply Idraet Pro White Emulsion (Niacinamide + Tranexamic Acid + Rice Proteins).
  • If skin feels dry or irritated, finish with Dermaglós Emulsión Hidratante after Pro White Emulsion absorbs.
